WELCOME TO CFE

Education that understands your child.

Every child learns differently. Our approach focuses on understanding each learner's strengths, identifying areas for improvement and creating a supportive learning journey.

We work with families to support academic development, confidence, independent learning and essential life skills. Our online programmes make learning accessible from different locations around the world.

  • Individual learning plans
  • One-to-one tutoring and small groups
  • Supportive and encouraging educators
  • Regular progress updates for families
